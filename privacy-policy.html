Privacy Policy — UPSC Prelims PYQ Practice

Last updated: September 7, 2026

UPSC Prelims PYQ Practice is an independent educational website and browser extension for UPSC CSE Prelims practice.

Data stored

The website stores practice progress, bookmarks, mistakes and preferences locally in the user's browser using local storage. The current website does not require an account.

Data we do not collect

The current version does not request or collect browsing history, website content, location, contacts, passwords or personal communications.

Data sharing

The current version does not transmit the locally stored practice data to an external server.

Cookies and analytics

The current V1 does not require analytics cookies or third-party tracking to provide its practice features. This policy will be updated if those services are introduced.

Advertising

The current V1 does not serve personalized advertisements. Future advertising, if introduced, will be described here before it is used.

Contact

For questions about privacy or the project, use the support contact published with the website or extension listing.

Independence

This project is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by the Union Public Service Commission.
